Its distinctive rounded form was created by the renowned Finnish designer Ristomatti Ratia. The heater brings together Nordic design, clever technology and the most important value of the old sauna culture – good löyly, the soft steam that rises from the stones.

Unlike an ordinary electric heater, Saunatonttu does not start warming up only when you decide to go to the sauna. Well insulated and closed with a lid, it keeps the heat in its stones, so the sauna is ready exactly when the feeling is right. Open the lid, pour water on the stones and let the warmth slowly fill the room.

The large mass of stones creates soft, deep and pleasantly moist steam. There is no need to chase high temperatures – Saunatonttu's best steam is born in a calm sauna of 60–65 °C, where breathing is easy and it feels good to stay longer.

This is luxury the Rabbit Hole Hotel way: not more noise and speed, but more time, warmth and presence. Sometimes setting the world a little more right begins simply with opening the sauna door.
