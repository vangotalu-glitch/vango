Our dear little [Water Guardian's Smoke Sauna](/en/ruumid/water-guardians-smoke-sauna/)
has been surveyed and added to the network.

Led by the Vana-Võromaa smoke sauna cooperative, a nationwide census of
Estonian smoke saunas has been gathering reports from all over the country.
By the start of autumn, 560 saunas had already been recorded — and ours is
now one of them.

You can browse where all these saunas are on the fresh
[smoke sauna map application](https://xgis.maaamet.ee/xgis2/page/app/suitsusaunad).
If your own sauna isn't listed yet, you can report it at
[www.savvusann.ee](https://www.savvusann.ee).

The Water Guardian's Smoke Sauna sits on its own islet in the middle of the
Wonderland's little lake — genuine and cosy, exactly what this register is
meant to celebrate.
