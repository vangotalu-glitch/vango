We went to test the ice that locals call the most boring time of the
year in Soomaa — but despite a bit of rain, the Tõramaa flooded meadows
were covered in a good, even layer of ice, and the kicksleds glided along
nicely!

We can lend kicksleds to one family straight from Vango; for a bigger
group, Soomaa.com can help you get out onto the ice.

Here's to more icy adventures and wonders!
