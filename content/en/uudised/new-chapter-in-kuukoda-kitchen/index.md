In September, the sound of hammering rang out from Kuukoda's kitchen. The
kitchen went through a thorough renovation, with our old friends at
Sinilind ehitus lending a hand. Thank you, Sinilind!

The kitchen now has every oven you could need, a dishwasher, and a new
spacious fridge with a freezer. There's enough counter space that one
person can roll out dough while another chops vegetables at the same time.
A third gets the honour of tasting whether the sauce needs one more pinch
of salt.

Once dinner is eaten, the dishes can go straight into the machine, leaving
more time to tell one more story around the table. Or two.
