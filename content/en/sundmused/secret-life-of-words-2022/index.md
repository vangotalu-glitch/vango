For the first time, Vango Wonderland hosted "The Secret Life of Words" — a
four-day creative camp for women only. Literary therapist Berit Kaschan and
creativity teacher Liina Kolk guided participants back to themselves and
their own voice through writing, reading, conversation, free-form drawing,
and other creative practices.

The camp's focus was on women's many roles, the courage to live life on
one's own terms, and finding and telling one's own story — each day
bringing its own theme and rhythm. The weekend unfolded amid Vango's calm
nature, where 25 women from different backgrounds gathered to write,
reflect, and support one another.

Thank you, Berit and Liina, and all the wonderful women who signed up —
see you next time!
