The Coastal Road Romantic Night 2023 concert unfolded in two acts. Early
in the evening, Alamakstud Härrad took the stage, filling the pine forest
with warm tunes from violin and accordion.

Later, under lantern light, Marek Sadam and Martin Trudnikov took over —
with the sky itself chipping in on bass. Powerful stuff!

Thank you to everyone who came out and made these evenings possible!
