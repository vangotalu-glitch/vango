In early October, Vango transformed for four days into an immersive fantasy
world — home to the international LARP "The Lesser Evil," which brought 70
participants into the world of the Witcher universe. The game featured 47
playable characters, with players and organisers joining from several
countries, including a large contingent from Finland.

Over four days and three nights, Vango's buildings and grounds became a
dark and atmospheric setting, where intrigue, alliances, and secrets played
out by lantern light and firelight.

Thank you to all the players and organisers who brought their story to
life at Vango — see you at the next adventure!
