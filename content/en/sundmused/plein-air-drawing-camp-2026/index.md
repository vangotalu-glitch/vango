In late August, the "Drink and Draw Estonia" crowd gathered at Vango for
three days of drawing in the open air. Over the weekend, participants
sketched both themed and life-drawing models — indoors and out, amid
Vango's fairytale-like nature.

The programme mixed indoor drawing on Friday evening with outdoor sessions
on the following two days, a hike in between, and a relaxing sauna and hot
tub on Saturday night. Evenings brought good company and unhurried dinners,
while the days were filled with creativity and discovery.

Thank you, Drink and Draw Estonia, and all the wonderful artists — see you
next time!
